# Recorded repair story

Reconstructed from earlier project conversations; dates are conversation anchors rather than a precise laboratory timeline.

1. Early September: firmware/sensor detection troubleshooting, connection/voltage checks and isolation of the controller by desoldering connections.
2. 5 September: smoke-stopper voltage drop and telemetry loss under throttle discussed. This is a recorded diagnostic context, not a complete root-cause certification.
3. Initial flight ended in a hard landing. A motor subsequently rubbed/caught at one point.
4. September repair account: replacement-motor troubleshooting exposed an ESC short; a failed MOSFET was identified using a multimeter.
5. A similar MOSFET salvaged from a used GTX 1050 Ti was fitted; the user reported the drone worked again.
6. 25 September: ESC repair completion and Liftoff training before returning to real flight were mentioned.

## What the record does not establish

Exact MOSFET markings, compatibility calculations, detailed measurements, extended load tests, current aircraft condition and measured flight performance were not recovered. This account does not certify ongoing electrical reliability or flight readiness.
